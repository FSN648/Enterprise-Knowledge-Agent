# Employee Handbook

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 paid annual leave days per calendar year.
Leave requests should normally be submitted at least five working days in advance.
Managers approve requests based on team coverage and business needs.

## Sick Leave

Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible when they are unable to work due to illness.

## Onboarding

New employees complete security training, product orientation, and development-environment setup during their first two weeks.
